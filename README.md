## 中文

### 一、总则
SongSum（"本应用"）是一款离线计算器与音乐玩具应用。我们**不要求注册账号、不连接自有服务器、不收集、不存储、不向任何第三方分享可识别您个人身份的数据**。您在应用内的录音、导入的音频文件、自选背景图片与各项设置，**全部仅保存在您的设备本地**，卸载应用即随之删除。

### 二、广告（仅 Android 版本）
本应用 **Android 版本**集成了 Google AdMob 广告（包括可选的激励视频广告：观看广告可获得临时功能解锁）。本应用 **iOS 版本当前不包含任何广告，也不进行跨应用跟踪**；如未来版本引入广告，本政策将同步更新。

AdMob 如何处理您的信息：
- AdMob 可能会使用设备标识符、网络信息与广告互动数据来投放广告。
- 在欧盟 / 欧洲经济区 / 英国境内，个性化广告需先经您明确同意；您可在 Android 系统设置中重置广告标识符或退出个性化广告。
- 详情见 Google 官方政策：<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> 与 <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

### 三、应用权限说明
- **麦克风**：用于录制自定义按键音效。录音仅在设备本地处理与保存，不会上传。
- **存储 / 文件**：用于导入自定义音频切片、保存您选择的背景图片与导出录音文件。相关数据仅存储在设备本地。

权限均由系统弹窗授权，您可随时在系统设置中撤销；撤销后仅对应功能不可用，不影响其他功能。

### 四、应用内购买
本应用提供一次性内购解锁（买断）。交易由 Google Play / App Store 处理，我们**无法获取也无需获取您的支付方式信息**；解锁状态仅记录在设备本地。

### 五、儿童与未成年人
本应用不针对 13 岁以下（或您所在司法辖区规定的最低年龄）儿童设计，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

## English

### 1. Overview
SongSum ("the App") is an offline calculator and music-toy app. We **do not require an account, do not operate servers, and do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable data**. All recordings, imported audio files, custom backgrounds, and settings are **stored only on your device** and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

### 2. Advertising (Android version only)
The **Android version** of the App integrates Google AdMob (including optional rewarded ads: watch an ad to unlock features temporarily). The **iOS version currently contains no ads and performs no cross-app tracking**. If a future version introduces ads, this policy will be updated accordingly.

How AdMob handles information:
- AdMob may use device identifiers, network information, and ad-interaction data to serve ads.
- In the EU / EEA / UK, personalized ads require your explicit consent; you can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads anytime in Android system settings.
- See Google's policies: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

### 3. App Permissions
- **Microphone**: used to record custom key sounds. Recordings are processed and saved locally only; they are never uploaded.
- **Storage / Files**: used to import custom audio for key slicing, save your chosen background image, and export recordings. All such data stays on your device.

All permissions are granted via system dialogs and can be revoked anytime in system settings; revoking a permission only disables the corresponding feature.

### 4. In-App Purchases
The App offers a one-time in-app purchase (lifetime unlock). Transactions are processed by Google Play / the App Store. **We neither receive nor need your payment information**; the unlock state is recorded on your device only.

### 5. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 6. Changes to This Policy
Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

### 7. Support & Contact
For technical support, feedback, or privacy-related requests, please contact us by email (we usually reply within 7 business days): **woliwala@gmail.com**
